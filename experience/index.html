01Socios En SaludGlobal Health and Economic Development InternSep. 2026 – PresentLima, Peru+

Working in Spanish across community health, program data, and treatment adherence in northern Lima.

I’m supporting community health programs through Dartmouth’s Dickey Center for International Understanding internship program. I began with an anemia prevention program in Pachacútec and now work with TB VIDA to understand how substance use, alcoholism, depression, and anxiety affect treatment adherence among patients with tuberculosis in Carabayllo and Comas in northern Lima.

The work spans different parts of Peru’s health system, from direct, in-person implementation to organizing and analyzing program data to support evaluation, donor reporting, and funding. I’ve been able to work in Spanish with health professionals, community leaders, and patients while seeing how the decisions made about a program translate into people’s everyday lives.

03Dartmouth CollegeEconomics Teaching AssistantJan. – Apr. 2026Hanover, NH+

Helping students make sense of microeconomic theory, mathematical problems, and graphical analysis.

I was selected as one of six teaching assistants from more than 100 applicants for Dartmouth’s introductory economics course. In office hours with around 30 students, I helped explain microeconomic theory, price mechanisms, and quantitative problems through mathematical and graphical analysis.

I enjoyed the process of finding a different way to explain something until it made sense to someone else. Teaching gave me another reason to understand the material well, and it was rewarding to watch students become more confident working through ideas that had initially felt difficult.
