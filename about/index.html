01 / A little more about me

A little more about me.

My name is Ivan Ramón Ochoa Lynch. I’m from Bloomington, Indiana, and I’m a student at Dartmouth College, where I’m pursuing a double major in Economics and Hispanic Studies, with a focus on development economics. I’m also a Division I athlete, competing in discus, hammer, and weight throw for Dartmouth’s track and field team.

As the son of two law professors, I’ve always been drawn to things that challenge me intellectually, give me something to chase, and make me feel passionate. I’ve found myself pursuing all sorts of interests throughout my life, sometimes ones that might not seem particularly connected to one another as I’m doing them. But I think there’s something wonderful about having so many things in the world worth being excited about, and I hope I never stop finding new ones.

I’ve been deeply involved in throwing since seventh grade, when my older brother broke the middle school record and I set out to break it. I had always been involved in sports, speech and debate, and other competitive activities, but the individual nature of track let me feel personally responsible for my own development.

Throwing is also deeply technical. It requires an understanding of the body and physical mechanics, which immediately meshed well with the way my brain works. I saw it as something I could understand intellectually while working to improve physically, a theme that continues in my life.

I also fell in love with coaching. Using the technical understanding and physical abilities I’ve developed, I’ve stepped into roles as a coach, mentor, teammate, captain, and friend to many people I’ve met along the way. I find that responsibility deeply rewarding. I love seeing people progress, smile, and laugh, and seeing the same passion I’ve felt for the sport take hold in my friends and students.

At Dartmouth, I’m a member of the Student-Athlete Advisory Committee, where I help lead Community Engagement and Philanthropy efforts to involve student-athletes in work that benefits our community and the world around us. I’m also a brother of Gamma Delta Chi and served as house manager this past summer, coordinating the budget and operations to maintain compliance and improve living conditions for the residents.

02 / What I believe

What I believe.

I strongly believe that every person on this planet is capable of things they might never have imagined for themselves, provided they have the right opportunities, time, and resources.

I care deeply about personal freedom and economic mobility. I want people to have the opportunity to pursue lives they find meaningful, take risks, chase ambitious ideas, and realize their own potential without being held back by circumstances beyond their control.

I think about this a lot, particularly in the context of economic development. The circumstances we’re born into, the institutions that surround us, and the resources we have access to can profoundly shape what we believe is possible for ourselves. I don’t think those circumstances should determine the limits of someone’s aspirations.

There are extraordinarily capable people all over the world with ideas, ambitions, and talents that may never be fully realized, not because they lack ability or determination, but because they lack access to the opportunities or resources necessary to pursue them. I want to spend my life working to change that.

I strongly believe that every person on this planet is capable of things they might never have imagined for themselves, provided they have the right opportunities, time, and resources.

03 / What I hope to build

What I hope to build.

Much of what I hope to accomplish professionally grows out of those beliefs.

I’m fascinated by the ways capital, institutions, and economic incentives shape the opportunities available to people. I want to build a career helping direct resources toward the people, businesses, and organizations capable of putting them to their greatest use.

Whether that means investing in promising enterprises, advising people who are building something worthwhile, helping organizations access the capital they need to grow, or working with institutions to make better decisions about how their resources are deployed, I want to contribute to an economy that creates real opportunities and improves people’s lives.

I also believe that good intentions and compelling stories don’t necessarily make for sustainable or effective organizations. Being able to distinguish a worthwhile business from one that simply tells a good story is incredibly important. I want to develop the analytical skills, financial judgment, and practical experience to make those distinctions and understand not only which ideas deserve support but also how to help them succeed.

I believe financial success and meaningful social impact can, and should, reinforce one another. Building profitable, sustainable organizations and creating value for the people they serve are not inherently competing ambitions. Some of the most exciting opportunities, at least to me, are those where the two work together.

Ultimately, I want to help mobilize the capital, resources, companies, and people who have the potential to make this world a better place. I don’t know exactly what form that work will take throughout my career, and I’m excited that it may lead me in directions I haven’t even considered yet.

Capital.
People.
Possibility.

04 / Beyond academics and work

Beyond academics and work.

Of course, there is much more to who I am than my academic interests or professional ambitions.

Athletics has been a huge part of my life. Throwing discus, hammer, and weight at the Division I level has taught me an enormous amount about discipline, patience, competition, and working toward something that may take years to achieve. There’s something incredibly rewarding about dedicating yourself to getting better at something, even when progress is slow, frustrating, or difficult to see.

I also love languages, literature, writing, traveling, adrenaline, and meeting people whose lives and experiences look nothing like my own. Studying Spanish and Hispanic cultures has given me new ways to think about the world, and living and working in Peru has allowed me to see some of the economic and social questions I’m interested in from a much more personal perspective.

I love conversations that leave me thinking differently, places that make me feel small in the best possible way, and people who are excited about what they’re doing. I enjoy being surrounded by people who challenge me, care deeply about something, and have their own ambitious ideas about what they want to make of their lives.

I hope to spend my life learning, growing, experiencing new things, and pursuing the things that make me excited to get out of bed in the morning.

05 / Why this website exists

Why this website exists.

I wanted to create a space that could bring together the different parts of my life, both professional and personal, without reducing me to just one of them.

Here you’ll find my academic and professional experiences, athletics, achievements, personal passions and adventures, academic and creative writing, and photographs that offer a glimpse into my day-to-day life.

Some of what you’ll find here reflects things I’ve already accomplished. Other parts represent interests I’m still exploring, questions I’m still asking, and ambitions I’m still figuring out how to pursue.

I hope these different pieces give you a better sense of who I am, what I care about, and the kind of life I’m trying to build.

I’m incredibly excited about where the world will take me, the wonderful and capable people I’ll meet, and the amazing things we will all accomplish together along the way.

Thanks for being here!
